Privacy

Last updated: October 6, 2026

This is a personal portfolio. It has no accounts, no forms and no advertising, and it sets no cookies. I don't collect or store personal information about you. The few services that keep the site running are described below.

Analytics

I use Plausible Analytics to see how many people visit and which pages they read. Plausible doesn't use cookies, doesn't follow you across other websites and doesn't store IP addresses. It records only aggregate information: the page visited, the referring site, the country, and the device and browser type. The data is stored in the EU.

Hosting

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages. Like any web server, GitHub may log visitors' IP addresses to keep its service secure.

The domain and traffic run through Cloudflare, which also serves some images and scripts. Cloudflare processes IP addresses to deliver the site and protect it from abuse.

I don't have access to these providers' logs, and their own privacy policies (linked above) cover how they handle that data.

Links to other sites

The site links to external pages such as GitHub, LinkedIn, ORCID and project sites. Once you follow a link, that site's own privacy policy applies.

Email

If you email me, I'll use your address and message only to reply to you.

Your rights

If you're in the EU or UK, the GDPR gives you the right to ask what data is held about you and to have it corrected or deleted. Since this site doesn't store personal data, there's usually nothing to hand over, but you're welcome to ask at hi@magdahamilton.dev.
