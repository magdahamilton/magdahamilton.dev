Privacy

Last updated October 6, 2026

This is a personal portfolio site. It doesn't use cookies, doesn't track you across other websites, and doesn't sell or share data for advertising. The short version of everything below: I only see anonymous, aggregate visitor counts.

Analytics

I use Plausible Analytics to see how many people visit and which pages they view. Plausible doesn't use cookies and doesn't collect personal data. It counts visits in aggregate (page views, referring sites, country, device type) without identifying individual visitors. You can read Plausible's data policy for details.

Services that handle your connection

Like any website, this one relies on a few providers to deliver it, and they process your IP address as part of normal operation:

  • GitHub Pages hosts the site. GitHub logs visitors' IP addresses for security purposes. See the GitHub Privacy Statement.
  • Cloudflare delivers the video files on this site and may handle network traffic to it. See the Cloudflare Privacy Policy.

I don't have access to these providers' logs and don't use them to identify anyone.

If you email me

If you contact me at hi@magdahamilton.dev, I'll use your name, address, and message only to reply. I don't add anyone to a mailing list. You can ask me to delete our correspondence at any time.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, including in the EU and UK, you may have the right to ask what personal data I hold about you, and to have it corrected or deleted. In practice, the only personal data I'd hold is email correspondence. To make a request, email hi@magdahamilton.dev.

Changes

If anything here changes, I'll update this page and the date at the top.
